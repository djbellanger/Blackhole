Upgrades

Spend mass

    Debris size and value follow the mass you are holding. Spending can drop you a tier, and smaller debris takes longer to earn back. Floors at Cars, City blocks, Moons and Solar systems stop the fall once you have reached them.

    Boost Shop

    Test mode: boosts are free and nothing is charged. Before you launch, set CFG.testMode to false and connect a payment provider.

    Each boost adds 25% to one ability. Boost time counts down only while you are playing, and a permanent boost never runs out.
